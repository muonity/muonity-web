Account Deletion — Farmia Fields

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Request removal of your Farmia Fields account and associated personal data. This page is public and requires no login.

Terms of Service · Privacy Policy

1. How to request deletion

Send an email to dev@muonity.com with the subject Farmia Fields — Account Deletion Request. State that you want to delete your Farmia Fields game account and associated personal data.

Email an account deletion request

  • Include your public Player ID and in-game nickname if available.
  • If useful for locating the account, identify the Google email linked to the game. Do not send your Google password or sign-in codes.
  • If you cannot access the game or do not know your Player ID, explain that in your email. You can still begin the request without reinstalling.

A public Player ID or nickname helps locate an account but is not proof of ownership. We may ask for proportionate additional verification before deleting data. Never send authentication tokens, purchase tokens, passwords, access codes or payment-card details. We will not ask for them.

2. Requests from the game

The current reviewed build has not yet activated its legal/deletion links, and an automated account-deletion operation has not been implemented. You can initiate a request through the email address on this page. We do not present a non-working in-game button as a completed deletion method.

An email request starts a support process; sending it does not instantly erase an account. We will explain any verification or further steps in our response.

3. Data covered by an account deletion request

After ownership verification, the request covers deletion of the account and associated personal data under Muonity’s control, subject to the limited retention exceptions below:

  • The Farmia Fields Firebase Authentication account and associated game-account identifiers.
  • Cloud farm progress in Firestore, including level, EXP, Coins, Gems, inventory, all farming areas, crops, trees, flowers, herbs, plots, decorations, achievements and reward records.
  • The public social profile, nickname/account links, friendship and request records, and related personally identifiable social activity. Related references in other players’ activity must also be handled appropriately.
  • Notification preferences, account-linked device and token registrations, pending notification work and associated personal delivery records.
  • Other linked personal game or support records, including relevant operation receipts where there is no justified reason to retain them.

Analytics and crash reports may use installation or app-instance identifiers rather than your public Player ID. We may need additional non-secret information to identify relevant data and use the provider’s deletion procedures. We will explain any information that cannot be linked or must be retained; deleting an Authentication user alone does not automatically remove all database or provider records.

4. Information that may be retained

Limited information may need to be retained to comply with a legal obligation, establish or defend legal claims, or address proportionate security and fraud-prevention needs. This does not authorize indefinite retention of the entire account or farm. Real-money purchases are currently disabled, so this page does not imply existing paid purchase records for your account.

We will explain any retained categories, reasons and relevant period or criteria in our response. No universal retention period has been established for all records. Notification history is scheduled for cleanup after seven days and stale device registrations after 30 inactive days, subject to asynchronous processing; those settings are not a deadline for completing your entire account-deletion request. Google service logs and backups may follow different deletion schedules.

5. Consequences, local data and Google

Deletion of confirmed game progress may be irreversible. A deleted farm, inventory, currency balance, achievements and social connections may not be recoverable. Signing in later may create a new identity; it does not entitle you to restoration of deleted progress.

Uninstalling the application or clearing local app data does not itself delete the cloud account. Conversely, a support request cannot remotely erase every local cache, backup or recovery copy on your devices. We will explain steps to avoid restoring old data after deletion. Device backup settings may affect local data restoration.

Deleting your game account does not delete your Google account or every record Google controls independently. Use Google’s privacy controls for those records. See the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Specific data requests and processing

You may ask to remove particular personal data without closing the whole account. Describe what you want removed and that you wish to keep playing. We will explain any effect on account or game functions. Disabling Usage Analytics affects future optional measurement; it is not itself a request to erase previously sent data.

We will respond to the email address you use and handle requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements. We may need to verify account ownership or a parent/guardian’s authority. Our response will explain the outcome, any limitations and any further steps. The absence of an automated deletion feature does not remove your legal rights.

We use the information in your request to locate the account, verify authority, respond and carry out the applicable action. Do not include unrelated personal information.
