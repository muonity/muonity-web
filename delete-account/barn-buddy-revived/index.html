Barn Buddy Revived – Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Barn Buddy Revived is developed by MUONITY (Muonity Studio), the independent project operated by Lukáš Pokorný.

You can request deletion of your game account and associated personal data by contacting us at dev@muonity.com. You do not need to sign in to this website or reinstall the game to submit a request.

Email an account deletion request

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to dev@muonity.com.
  2. Use the subject: Barn Buddy Revived – Account Deletion Request.
  3. State that you wish to delete your Barn Buddy Revived account and associated personal game data.
  4. Include your Player ID or Firebase account UID if you have it, and your farm display name. Tell us whether your game account is anonymous or linked to Google, if known. These details help us locate the correct account.
  5. We may request additional, proportionate verification to confirm that you are the legitimate account holder before deleting data.

If you do not know your Player ID or cannot access the game, you can still email us to begin the request. We will help determine what non-secret information is needed. A Player ID or farm name helps identify an account but does not, by itself, prove ownership.

Never send your Google password, authentication tokens, access codes, payment card details or other secret sign-in credentials. We will not ask for them. Do not send unrelated identity documents unless a necessary, proportionate verification step has been explained to you.

For an unlinked anonymous account, contact us before clearing app data if possible: losing the device's account credentials can make verification more difficult. This does not prevent you from submitting a request after uninstalling.

What will be deleted

After verifying ownership and confirming the scope of your request, we will delete the following associated data under Muonity's control, except for information that must be retained for the limited reasons explained below:

  • Your Barn Buddy Revived Firebase Authentication account and associated game-account identifiers and links.
  • Your saved cloud farm in Cloud Firestore, including level, EXP, Buddy Coins, Buddy Gems, inventory, plants, trees, plots, timers and other saved farm progress.
  • Related personal game records linked to that account, including its farm profile, display name, saved activity and operation or reward records.
  • Other associated personal information controlled by Muonity, including relevant support records where they do not need to be retained.

For personal information processed on our behalf by service providers, we will use the applicable deletion procedures. Analytics and crash-reporting records may use installation or app-instance identifiers instead of the game account UID, so we may need additional non-secret information to locate relevant records. We will explain any limitations rather than promise that deleting an account automatically removes every service record.

Account deletion removes access to the associated cloud farm, and deleted progress may not be recoverable. Signing in again may create a new game account; it does not restore the deleted farm.

Local saves and Google-managed information

An email request cannot remotely erase saves, settings or recovery copies stored only on your device. You can remove these through Android's app-data controls or by uninstalling the game. Device backup and restore settings may affect whether local data returns. Clearing local data alone does not delete your Firebase account or cloud farm.

Deleting your Barn Buddy Revived account does not delete your Google account. Google controls some account, platform and service records independently. You can manage those through Google's data and privacy controls; see also the Google Privacy Policy. Our response will identify any further steps relevant to your request.

What data may be retained

We may retain limited information where a legal or accounting obligation requires it, or where necessary and justified for security, fraud prevention or establishing or defending legal claims. This is not a reason to retain your entire farm or account indefinitely.

The current game implementation does not offer real-money purchases. This page does not imply that Muonity already holds purchase or accounting records for your game account.

We have not established a single fixed retention period for every category. If an exception applies to your request, we will explain which information is retained, why, and the applicable period or the criteria used to determine it. Retention will be limited to the relevant requirement or purpose. Service-provider backup and deletion processes may also affect when removal is completed; we do not promise instant deletion from every backup.

Requesting deletion of specific data

You may request deletion of particular personal information without asking to close your entire account. Email dev@muonity.com, name Barn Buddy Revived, identify the account as described above and specify what you want removed. We will explain any effect on gameplay or account functionality before proceeding where necessary.

Turning Settings → Usage analytics and crash reports OFF changes future optional measurement. It does not by itself delete information already sent to the services. If your request concerns that information, please say so.

How we process your request

We will reply to the email address you use to contact us, carry out any necessary ownership verification and handle your request in accordance with applicable legal requirements. Our response will explain the outcome, any information retained and any additional steps needed.

If you are acting as a parent or guardian, say so in your request. We may need to verify your authority without asking for unnecessary personal information.

We use the information you provide to locate the account, verify and fulfill the request, respond to you and meet applicable obligations. Sending a request does not sign you up for marketing.
