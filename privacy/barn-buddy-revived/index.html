Last updated: October 27, 2026

See also the Terms of Service.

It is not a separate company.

This policy covers the game, including its current testing version. It does not imply that planned features are available. Our Terms of Service explain the rules for using the game. Using the game or accepting those terms does not itself give consent to optional measurement or personalized advertising.

2. Accounts and Google sign-in

Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous game account with a unique identifier. “Anonymous” means you do not have to provide a name or email to start an account; it does not mean that its game data or technical identifiers are anonymous personal data.

You may voluntarily link the account to Google. Google and Firebase then process sign-in information, the associated identifiers and authentication tokens, and account information such as your Google display name and email where supplied by the sign-in service. Google also processes technical authentication information, including IP addresses, to operate and protect the service. We do not receive your Google password.

Linking allows you to access the same cloud farm on another device. An unlinked anonymous account may not be recoverable after clearing app data, uninstalling the game or losing the device. Sign-in and initial cloud loading require a connection. Authentication is separate from optional analytics.

3. Farm progress, cloud saves and offline storage

Cloud Firestore stores your farm under your Firebase account identifier. The save includes your farm display name, level, EXP, Buddy Coins, Buddy Gems, inventory, plants, trees, plots, objects, timers and relevant gameplay state. It also includes save revisions and timestamps, recent farm activity and operation or reward records used to preserve progress and avoid duplicate processing.

The game keeps account-specific local saves, recovery copies and settings on your device. After your farm has been loaded, supported own-farm activities can be played offline and synchronized when a connection is available. Conflicting changes on two devices are not automatically merged: the game can preserve a local branch while you choose how to continue. Local recovery copies can remain on the device.

This processing provides the game, synchronization and recovery. The current cloud-save design is private to the account; it does not publish your farm to other players. Online social visits and shared multiplayer features are not currently provided by this cloud-save implementation.

4. Optional usage analytics and crash reports

Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics are integrated. Optional measurement is OFF by default. On first use, the game does not treat account creation, a Google sign-in or an advertising choice as permission for analytics.

You can enable or disable Settings → Usage analytics and crash reports. This is a single control for optional usage measurement and crash reporting. Your preference is stored locally and restored on later launches; it is not a cloud-synchronized consent setting.

The current implementation enables collection only when this preference is enabled and the advertising privacy check has completed with permission to request ads. That additional technical condition is not itself analytics consent. Leaving the measurement switch OFF does not prevent core farming or account synchronization.

When enabled, usage measurement can include app sessions, device and app information, app-instance identifiers, and events such as opening the game, planting and harvesting crops, obtaining seeds, leveling up, using fertilizer and ad interactions. Seed-related events refer to in-game activity, not real-money purchases. Our custom event parameters are limited to defined game items, quantities and levels; we do not intentionally send player names, email addresses, account IDs, sign-in tokens or free-form player text in these events. We do not set an Analytics User-ID.

Crashlytics can process crash traces, installation identifiers, app and device information and selected technical error codes to diagnose stability problems. This technical information is not necessarily anonymous. With reporting disabled, the SDK may retain crash information locally rather than upload it; enabling reporting can allow pending reports to be sent.

Turning the switch OFF disables further optional collection, resets local Analytics data and requests deletion of unsent Crashlytics reports. It does not retroactively erase information already received by Google. Analytics advertising storage, advertising user-data consent and advertising personalization remain denied in this integration.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

The game integrates Google AdMob rewarded-ad support and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The current testing configuration uses Google's test rewarded-ad unit; production advertisements are not enabled. A particular reward or commercial advertising offer should not be inferred from the presence of this integration.

Where an optional rewarded-ad opportunity is available, viewing it is voluntary. Ad services can process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from network information, app and device identifiers where permitted, ad interactions and diagnostic or fraud-prevention information. A test or non-personalized ad does not mean that no technical information is processed.

UMP checks advertising privacy requirements at startup and presents the configured form when required. Ad initialization and preloading depend on UMP allowing ad requests, which can rely on a valid previous choice. An ad may therefore be loaded before you choose to watch it. Settings → Ad Privacy is available when UMP requires a privacy-options entry point. Changing an advertising choice discards previously loaded ads.

Advertising choices and the optional measurement switch have different purposes. An advertising choice alone does not enable Firebase usage analytics. Advertising availability depends on your settings, region and the service.

6. Google Play and future features

Google Play distributes the Android game and processes information under Google's own terms and privacy policies. The current game does not provide real-money purchases of Buddy Gems or other items. Local development payment simulations are not a production purchase service.

If real-money purchases or social features are introduced, we will explain the relevant processing and update this policy before those features require it. This policy does not claim that a payment-verification backend, public leaderboard or social-sharing service is currently part of Barn Buddy Revived.

9. Retention and security

Local progress, settings and recovery files remain on your device until removed through available app controls or by clearing app data or uninstalling. Removing local data does not by itself delete your cloud farm or Firebase account.

Account and cloud-save data are kept to provide your account and restore progress, subject to valid deletion requests. Analytics, diagnostic and service records are subject to the applicable Google service settings and retention policies. We do not claim a fixed retention period that has not been established. Information needed for legal obligations or proportionate security purposes may be retained for those purposes.

Firebase services use encrypted network connections. Authentication and access controls are intended to restrict cloud saves to the relevant account. Local saves depend on your device's security; they are not a guarantee of end-to-end encryption. No storage or transmission system is completely secure.

10. Your choices, rights and deletion requests

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion or portability of your personal data, restriction of processing, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent and complain to the competent data protection authority. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before it.

To request deletion of your Barn Buddy Revived account and associated data, or to request deletion of particular information without closing the account, email dev@muonity.com. Identify the game and describe your request. You may include your farm display name to help us locate the account. We may ask for proportionate verification; a display name alone may not uniquely identify an account. Never provide your Google password or access tokens.

Requests can concern your Firebase game account, its identifiers and the associated cloud farm and personal information under our control. We will respond to your contact email and explain any applicable limits or necessary retention. Deletion of game information does not delete your Google account or all records controlled independently by Google. You can also use Google's data and privacy controls.

There is currently no in-game automated account-deletion control. Contact us before clearing an unlinked anonymous account, because losing its local credentials can make ownership verification and recovery difficult. We do not promise recovery of deleted progress.

11. Children and young players

Privacy protections for children depend on applicable law, the player's age and the services used. A store age rating is not itself consent to data processing, and an advertising privacy message does not by itself establish parental authorization.

We do not ask children to submit personal contact information through gameplay. Where a parent or guardian's authorization is legally required, a child's action alone is not sufficient. Optional measurement should remain off unless the necessary authorization can be provided. Parents or guardians can contact dev@muonity.com with a concern or a deletion request.

The current testing integration should not be understood as a declaration that child-directed advertising or age-verification controls are in place. The applicable audience and service restrictions must be confirmed for the release being distributed.
