Pizza Worm – Account & Data Deletion

Pizza Worm is developed by MUONITY (Muonity Studio), the independent project operated by Lukáš Pokorný.

You can request deletion of your Pizza Worm game account and associated personal data by email. You do not need to sign in to this website or reinstall the game to submit a request.

Email an account deletion request

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to dev@muonity.com.
  2. Use the subject: Pizza Worm – Account Deletion Request.
  3. State that you wish to delete your Pizza Worm account and associated personal data.
  4. Provide your Google Play Games player name, if available, to help identify the account.
  5. We may request additional verification to ensure that the request comes from the legitimate account holder.

A player name helps us locate an account but does not by itself prove ownership. If you cannot access the game or do not know your player name, you can still email us to start the request.

Never send your Google account password, payment card details, authentication or purchase tokens, or account access codes. We will not ask you to share them.

What data can be deleted

After verifying your request and identifying the relevant records, account deletion covers the following data under Muonity Studio's control, subject to the limited retention reasons described below:

  • Your Pizza Worm Firebase Authentication account and its associated application identity records. This is not deletion of your Google account.
  • Related account records held in our backend or Firebase, including identifiable account links and purchase-ownership records where they do not need to be retained.
  • Other personal information we control that can be linked to your verified request, including relevant support records, subject to applicable retention requirements.

For information processed on our behalf by service providers, we will use their available deletion procedures as applicable. Some analytics records use app-instance identifiers rather than your player name, so we may need additional non-secret information to identify the relevant records.

Deleting a game identity can affect online sign-in and access to linked purchase entitlements. We will explain any relevant consequences when processing your request. Deletion does not automatically refund purchases or delete Google's transaction records. Signing in to the game again after deletion may create a new game identity.

Google Play Games and other Google-managed data

Google manages Play Games profiles and leaderboard data. We will help you with associated leaderboard information where supported, but removing a Firebase account does not automatically erase Google Play Games scores. Hiding a leaderboard entry is not the same as deleting it.

You can request removal of Pizza Worm's Play Games data through Google's controls. Follow Google's instructions for deleting data for an individual game and select Pizza Worm. You do not need to delete your entire Google account.

Google-managed payment, account and service records are governed by Google's applicable policies and controls. See the Google Privacy Policy. Removing Play Games data does not itself remove records held by Muonity; the email request above covers our processing.

What data may be retained

Some purchase, transaction, entitlement or security records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or for justified fraud-prevention and security purposes. This can include limited purchase references and revocation records used to prevent invalid purchases from being reused.

These exceptions do not mean that all account data must be kept. We will explain any applicable retained categories and reasons in our response. Retention depends on the relevant purpose and applicable requirements; we do not state an unverified fixed retention period.

Deleting data without deleting your account

You may also email dev@muonity.com to request deletion of specific personal data while keeping your account. State which data you want removed and that you wish to retain the account. We will verify the request, explain what can be removed and describe any effect on the features you use.

Turning Usage Analytics OFF stops future configured analytics collection but does not delete previously sent data. Please identify analytics deletion as part of your request if that is what you want.

Game saves and settings stored only on your device are not remotely removed by an email request. You can remove local app data through Android's app settings or uninstall the game; device backup and restore settings may affect whether it returns. This does not by itself delete your online account or backend records.

Processing requests

We will reply to the email address you use to contact us and handle your request in accordance with applicable legal requirements. Our reply will explain any necessary identity verification, the outcome, and any information retained or further steps needed for Google-managed data.

If you are a parent or guardian making a request for a child, please say so. We may need to verify your authority without requesting unnecessary personal information.
