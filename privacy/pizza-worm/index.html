Privacy Policy — Pizza Worm

Effective Date: October 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Muonity Studio ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes Pizza Worm, a mobile game for Android devices. Muonity Studio is the name used for the independent project operated by Lukáš Pokorný.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how information is processed when you use our game.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, why it is processed, which third-party services may be involved, and what choices and rights you have.

Using Pizza Worm does not itself constitute consent to optional analytics or personalized advertising. Advertising choices and the separate analytics setting are explained below, including the analytics preference used in version 1.0.1.

Online features depend on the installed build and service configuration. In the game configuration covered by this policy, Google Play Billing, Play Games Services, AdMob and Firebase Analytics are not activated. The sections below also explain the information these integrations handle when enabled.

2. Information We Process

Pizza Worm may process the following categories of information, depending on the features you use, their availability, and your privacy choices.

Game Progress and Preferences

The game stores certain information locally on your device, including:

  • Current and saved game progress.
  • Scores and personal records.
  • Selected worm skin and tablecloth.
  • Game settings.
  • Sound and music preferences.
  • Relevant gameplay state.
  • Privacy preferences where applicable.

This information is used to operate the game and preserve your progress. Local gameplay saves are not automatically uploaded to Muonity Studio servers. Separate leaderboard submissions and analytics events, when enabled, are described below.

Google Play Games Services

If you use Google Play Games Services, Google may process information necessary to provide player authentication, leaderboards and related gaming functionality. This may include:

  • Your Google Play Games player identifier.
  • Your public player name.
  • Your leaderboard scores.
  • Authentication and service-related information.

Leaderboard information may be visible to other players according to Google Play Games settings and the availability of the service. Google processes information under its own privacy policies.

In-App Purchases

Pizza Worm supports optional purchases of cosmetic worm skins and tablecloths through Google Play Billing when the purchase service is enabled. Purchase-related information may include:

  • Purchased product identifiers.
  • Purchase tokens and transaction status.
  • Signed purchase receipts and information needed to validate purchases.
  • Information required to restore purchased items.

Payments are processed by Google Play. Muonity Studio does not receive or store your full payment card details.

The current integration verifies signed purchase receipts on the device and stores receipts and handled purchase tokens in private application storage to maintain purchase entitlements. It uses Google Play to query and acknowledge purchases. It does not send purchase tokens to a separate Muonity verification server.

Advertising

Pizza Worm may display optional rewarded advertisements that allow players to continue a game after a collision. Advertising is provided through Google AdMob when enabled.

Depending on your region, age-related settings and consent choices, advertising services may process information such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers where permitted.
  • IP address.
  • Approximate location derived from network information.
  • Advertising interaction information.
  • Diagnostic and fraud-prevention information.

Advertising data processing is subject to applicable privacy requirements and Google's policies. The advertising integration uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain current consent information and show a consent form where required. It requests ads only when UMP permits ad requests.

Declining optional advertising consent does not prevent access to the core game. However, the availability of rewarded advertisements may vary; an ad is not guaranteed after declining personalization or other consent choices.

Firebase Analytics

Pizza Worm includes an integration for Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how players interact with the game and improve gameplay. Depending on service configuration and your analytics setting, analytics may include:

  • Game session activity.
  • Game starts, saved or resumed games, and completed runs.
  • Session duration.
  • Scores and gameplay progression.
  • Pizza collection and other gameplay events.
  • Cosmetic selection events.
  • Advertising and purchase-related events.
  • Technical application and device information, including information collected automatically by the SDK when active.

We do not intentionally include your name, email address or other directly identifying personal information in custom gameplay analytics events. Custom events do not include purchase tokens or leaderboard player names. Firebase may use application-instance or device-related identifiers; analytics should not be understood as necessarily anonymous.

Firebase Analytics is not activated in the game configuration covered by this policy. In version 1.0.1, the game's analytics preference defaults to ON when no saved choice exists; an existing OFF choice is preserved. If Firebase is configured and activated, this ON preference can enable analytics without a separate opt-in. The default ON setting is not a record of explicit consent.

You can switch analytics OFF or ON through Settings → Privacy Settings. Switching OFF stops further configured analytics collection; custom events while OFF are discarded rather than queued for later. It does not delete previously transmitted data. Advertising choices are managed separately.

3. How We Use Information

Information is processed, where the relevant feature is available, to:

  • Operate and maintain the game.
  • Save gameplay progress.
  • Provide online leaderboards.
  • Process and restore purchases.
  • Deliver optional rewarded advertisements.
  • Understand gameplay performance where analytics is permitted.
  • Detect errors and prevent fraud.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell players' personal information.

4. Third-Party Services

Pizza Worm includes integrations for the following Google services. Their availability depends on the game configuration:

  • Google Play Games Services: player authentication and leaderboards.
  • Google Play Billing: in-app purchases and purchase restoration.
  • Google AdMob: optional rewarded advertisements.
  • Google User Messaging Platform: advertising privacy and consent management.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: gameplay analytics and application usage statistics, controlled by the separate analytics setting described above.

These services are operated by Google and may process information under their respective terms and privacy policies. For more information, visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase
  • Google AdMob Help

5. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or similar privacy legislation applies, processing may be based on:

  • Contractual necessity: to provide gameplay features and services requested by the player.
  • Consent: for optional analytics and advertising processing where required. An automatically enabled preference does not itself constitute that consent.
  • Legitimate interests: for appropriate security, fraud prevention and essential service operation, subject to applicable law.
  • Legal obligations: where processing is necessary to comply with legal requirements.

The applicable legal basis depends on the specific processing activity and the relevant jurisdiction.

6. Advertising and Analytics Choices

Pizza Worm provides separate controls for analytics and, when configured and available, advertising privacy. You can access the relevant options through Settings → Privacy Settings.

Advertising choices are managed through Google's UMP mechanisms. The analytics ON/OFF setting is independent of the advertising form. Version 1.0.1 uses ON for a missing analytics preference; it does not treat that default as an explicit user choice.

You can play the core game with analytics OFF. Turning it OFF stops future configured analytics collection. Changing advertising choices affects future ad requests according to the relevant service's controls. Some previously processed information may remain subject to applicable retention requirements.

7. Data Retention

Locally stored game progress and preferences generally remain on your device until removed through applicable game controls, application data deletion or uninstalling the application. Device or platform backup and restore settings may affect whether local data is restored.

Information processed by Google services is retained according to the applicable service settings, policies and legal requirements. This policy does not specify an unverified retention period for Google's servers.

Muonity Studio does not independently retain local gameplay saves on its own servers. Deleting local application data does not revoke purchases or automatically delete information already processed by Google. Contact us for information about the applicable retention settings.

8. Data Security

The game uses local application storage and checks signed Google Play purchase receipts before granting paid entitlements. These measures help protect the relevant game and purchase information; they do not make a device or its stored data tamper-proof.

Third-party services provide their own security controls. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. International Data Processing

Google services may process information in countries outside your country of residence. Where applicable, international transfers are subject to the safeguards and legal mechanisms provided by the relevant service providers.

See Google's information about international data transfers and its Privacy Policy for further information.

10. Children's Privacy

Pizza Worm is designed as a casual game. A casual visual style does not by itself determine the intended age group.

The advertising integration supports child-directed and under-age-of-consent settings. Advertising remains disabled in the configuration covered by this policy while the audience configuration is incomplete. This policy does not represent that a final Google Play target audience or AdMob age configuration has been verified.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal contact information through the game. If you believe information concerning a child has been processed inappropriately, please contact us.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of applicable personal information.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. Some information is processed directly by Google, and requests concerning that information may need to be submitted through Google's privacy controls. This does not remove rights you may have in relation to our own processing.

For privacy questions relating to Muonity Studio, contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when game features, third-party services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

The effective date will be updated when appropriate. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request renewed consent.
