Privacy Policy — Bait Dash

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Android package: studio.muonity.baitdash

1. Who we are

Bait Dash is developed and published by Muonity, an independent project operated by Lukáš Pokorný. Lukáš Pokorný is the controller of personal information processed for the purposes described here, except where Google processes information independently under its own policies.

This policy covers Bait Dash for Android. Contact: dev@muonity.com. Website: muonity.com.

2. Local game data

The game stores progress, settings, personal records and purchase entitlements locally on your device to operate the game and preserve your progress. This local storage is separate from leaderboard submissions, purchase verification, advertising and optional analytics, which involve the online services described below.

Clearing app data or uninstalling may remove local saves. It does not automatically erase information already held by Google or our purchase-verification backend.

3. Analytics

Bait Dash uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand application usage and gameplay events and improve the game. Analytics is activated after you give consent, not merely because you install or open the game. Analytics consent is separate from advertising choices.

When enabled, analytics may process app-instance identifiers, sessions, interactions and gameplay events, together with technical information such as app version, device model, operating system and language. These identifiers should not be treated as making all analytics information anonymous.

You may decline optional analytics or withdraw your consent. Contact dev@muonity.com for help changing your choice or requesting deletion. Withdrawal affects future consent-based processing; it does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful or automatically erase previously collected information.

See Firebase privacy and security information.

4. Advertising and consent

Bait Dash uses Google AdMob exclusively for voluntary rewarded advertisements. You choose whether to watch an available advertisement in exchange for an in-game reward. Watching an advertisement is not, by itself, consent to personalized advertising or optional analytics.

Advertising services may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from network information, device or advertising identifiers where permitted, ad interactions and diagnostic or fraud-prevention information. Processing depends on applicable settings, your region and privacy choices. Non-personalized advertising can still involve data processing.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages advertising consent where required. You can revisit advertising choices through the relevant option in the game's settings. Refusing optional advertising consent does not require you to stop playing, although rewarded-ad availability may vary. Advertising choices do not themselves change your separate analytics choice.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps and sites using its services.

5. Google Play Games Services

Google Play Games Services provides player sign-in and the global High Score and Longest Distance leaderboards. Google may process your player identifier, public player name, submitted scores or distances, and authentication and service information needed for these features.

Your player name and submitted leaderboard results may be visible to other players according to the service's settings. Google account and Play Games information is also governed by Google's policies and privacy controls.

6. Purchases and server verification

Google Play Billing processes purchases of skins, the all-skins bundle, bonuses and extra lives. Google handles payments; Muonity does not receive your full payment card details.

Purchase tokens and product or transaction information are processed by our verification backend on Google Cloud Run. Cloud Firestore stores transaction-verification and fulfillment records to prevent the same purchase from granting products more than once. Purchase references may be linkable to a transaction or player and are not necessarily anonymous.

This processing supports delivery of purchased products and protects transactions against duplication and abuse. Google Cloud services may also process technical request and security information. Declining analytics does not disable processing necessary to verify a purchase you request.

7. Purposes and legal bases

Where the GDPR applies, processing is based on contractual necessity to provide requested game features, leaderboard services and purchased products; consent for optional analytics and advertising processing where required; and legitimate interests in protecting the game and purchase system against fraud and abuse, subject to your rights. Processing may also be necessary to meet applicable legal obligations.

Consent to optional processing is not inferred from simply using the game. You may withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Information you send when contacting us is used to answer your request, verify it where necessary and meet relevant obligations.

8. Recipients and international processing

Google provides Firebase Analytics, AdMob, UMP, Google Play Games Services, Google Play Billing, Google Cloud Run and Cloud Firestore. Information is disclosed to the relevant services for the features described above. Public leaderboard information can also be seen by other players. Information may be disclosed where required by law.

Google may process information outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Applicable service terms and transfer safeguards govern this processing. See Google's Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information and Google's international data-transfer information.

9. Retention and security

Local progress and preferences generally remain until removed through app controls, device settings or uninstalling, subject to device backup behavior. Backend purchase records are retained according to the need to verify entitlements, prevent duplicate grants, resolve transaction issues and meet applicable obligations. Retention of technical logs and analytics depends on the relevant service configuration and purpose.

Information held by Google is also subject to its service settings and policies. This policy does not specify an unverified fixed retention period. Contact us for details about information relating to you and applicable deletion options.

Server-side purchase verification helps protect purchase entitlements. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Your choices, rights and deletion requests

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction of processing or data portability, object to processing based on legitimate interests, withdraw consent, and complain to a competent data protection authority.

Send privacy or deletion requests to dev@muonity.com and identify Bait Dash. We may ask for information necessary to locate relevant records and verify that the request is yours. Do not send Google passwords, payment card details or access tokens.

Requests concerning information controlled by Muonity are assessed under applicable law. Some transaction or security records may need to be retained for applicable obligations or legitimate purposes. Information controlled independently by Google may require a request through Google's privacy controls. Deleting game-related information does not delete your Google account.

11. Children's privacy

Age-related requirements and parental-consent rules depend on the player's location and the services used. Where a parent or guardian's authorization is required by applicable law, a child's own choice does not replace that authorization. If you believe a child's information has been processed inappropriately, contact us so we can investigate and address the request.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when game features, services or privacy practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request new consent.
